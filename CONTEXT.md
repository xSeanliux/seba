# Seba

A long-term personal tutor: it decides what a learner should review and learn
next, and remembers what happened between sessions.

## Language

**Goal**:
Something a learner is working toward over many sessions, such as understanding
probability or digesting the literature around a research question.
_Avoid_: Course, project

**Direction**:
The learner's current statement of what a goal is for. It can change while the
goal is under way, and what is being learnt changes with it.
_Avoid_: Topic, subject (a subject is the kind of material, not the aim)

**Concept**:
One idea a learner can come to understand, sized to a few sessions. Concepts
depend on one another, and those dependencies are the curriculum.
_Avoid_: Topic, lesson, paper

**Source**:
Material a concept is taught from: a paper, a chapter, a page. A source can
teach many concepts and a concept can draw on many sources.
_Avoid_: Treating a paper as a concept

**Card**:
One question with one answer, belonging to exactly one concept, reviewed on a
spaced schedule. A concept usually has several.
_Avoid_: Item, flashcard
